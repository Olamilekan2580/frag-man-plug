VEGArance for merchants

Create a store that feels like yours.

Set up your brand, add products, and share your storefront. Connect your own payment and supplier accounts, then build your collection.

What you get

Your own storefronts. Create up to five stores. Each store gets its own storefront at /shops/your-store.

Your own provider connections. Connect your own CJ Dropshipping account for supplier products, your own Flutterwave account for payments, and your own Resend account for receipt emails. Secrets are saved privately and never shown again.

Supplier search. Search CJ’s US catalog, import drafts, review product details, and check supplier stock before anything is offered for sale.

Test checkout. Build your catalog and test customer checkout with your own Flutterwave test account. Test orders can send receipt emails and request test refunds.

What is not live yet

Live merchant payments are not enabled yet. Supplier submission and funding are blocked for test payments. The connection check confirms API read access only — it does not confirm USD approval or enable checkout.

No earnings are promised: what a store earns depends on its own products, prices, and customers.
