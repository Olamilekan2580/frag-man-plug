Our Story

The Plug for Real
Fragrance.

A fragrance can change how you feel. Finding yours should be an adventure, without a shelf full of blind buys.

So we became the plug: an extensive collection of authentic fragrances — Armaf, Lattafa, Rasasi, Afnan, Jo Milano Paris and more — hand-poured into 3ml, 5ml and 10ml decants, shipped from Florida. Full bottles for the ones you fall in love with, and custom hybrid mixes for those who want something no one else has.

Based in Florida, The Frag Man Plug brings discovery closer: sample different houses, explore your favorites, or create a blend of two scents.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked

The business offers decants of authentic branded fragrances. A decant is a smaller portion transferred into a sample vial, rather than an original retail bottle.

Choose 3ml, 5ml or 10ml in this demo. Spray counts and wear time vary with the atomizer and how much you use. Full-bottle options will be confirmed for launch.

Pick any two fragrances from the vault as your base and accent, set your ratio (70/30, 60/40 or 50/50) and choose a size. Preview your combination and add it to the demo cart. Final blend pricing and fulfillment details will be confirmed for launch.

The business ships across the US from Florida. This demo uses an illustrative $6.95 shipping rate with free shipping from $75; final rates and delivery times need confirmation.

The final return policy will be confirmed before the store launches. This is a demonstration, and no payments or real orders are accepted.

Full bottles are part of the business offering. Bottle sizes, availability and prices have not been supplied yet, so this demo lets you explore decants first.
