Our Story

Born in Florida.
Worn Everywhere.

The Frag Man Plug started with a simple frustration: great fragrance shouldn't require a leap of faith — or a full-bottle price tag. Blind-buying a $120 bottle you might hate? That's a tax on curiosity.

So we built the plug: authentic decants of the scents worth knowing, full bottles of the ones worth keeping, and custom hybrid mixes for those who want something no one else has. Every order is packed by hand, every decant is poured fresh from sealed stock, and every mix is blended to your exact ratio.

This is fragrance without the gatekeeping — luxury that starts at $11, not $110.
