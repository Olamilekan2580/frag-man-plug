Our Story

The Plug for Real
Fragrance.

The Frag Man Plug started with a simple frustration: the best Middle Eastern fragrances were hard to try before you buy. Blind-buying a full bottle you might hate? That's a tax on curiosity.

So we became the plug: 215+ authentic fragrances from 23 houses — Armaf, Lattafa, Rasasi, Afnan, Jo Milano Paris and more — hand-poured into 3ml, 5ml and 10ml decants, shipped from Florida. Full bottles for the ones you fall in love with, and custom hybrid mixes for those who want something no one else has.

Every decant is poured fresh from sealed stock when you order. No fakes, no watered-down mystery juice — just the real thing, at a price that lets you explore.
